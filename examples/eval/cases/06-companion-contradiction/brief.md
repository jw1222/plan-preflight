# Subscription Pause — Product Brief (locked)

Decided with product, finance, and support. The plan must conform to this
brief; changes to these points go back to the brief, not into the plan.

- A subscriber may pause for 1, 2, or 3 months, chosen at pause time.
- During a pause there is no billing and the subscriber keeps **read-only**
  access to their content.
- The billing anchor date is preserved: after the pause, billing resumes on
  the same day-of-month as before.
- The billing provider stays BillCo; pauses are implemented on our side,
  not as provider-side subscription changes.
- One pause per billing year.
